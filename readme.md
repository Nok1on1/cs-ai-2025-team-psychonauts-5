## About
Dialogos is an innovative educational platform designed to empower university entrance exam candidates through intelligent preparation and deep learning. By leveraging a curated archive of previous years' university entrance exams, Dialogos uses advanced AI to generate adaptive, high-quality mock tests tailored to modern academic standards.

But Dialogos is more than just test prep — it’s a space for critical thinking and meaningful learning. Inspired by the Socratic method, Dialogos encourages students to engage in thoughtful dialogue, ask deeper questions, and explore concepts rather than just memorize answers. This approach cultivates a habit of analytical reasoning and intellectual curiosity, essential for academic success and lifelong learning.

Whether you're aiming to sharpen your test-taking skills or deepen your understanding through inquiry-based learning, Dialogos provides a personalized, thoughtful, and future-ready preparation journey.
